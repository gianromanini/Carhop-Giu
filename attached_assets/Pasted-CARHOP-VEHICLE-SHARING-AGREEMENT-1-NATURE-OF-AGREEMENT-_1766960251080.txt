CARHOP VEHICLE SHARING AGREEMENT
1. NATURE OF AGREEMENT This Vehicle Sharing Agreement ("Agreement") is between CarHop Rental Services ("Platform") and the individual identified in the booking confirmation ("Guest"). The Guest acknowledges that CarHop is a vehicle sharing platform facilitating the temporary use of a vehicle. This is not a lease.

2. RENTAL PERIOD & PAYMENT The Guest agrees to pay the total charges quoted, including rental fees, taxes, and applicable fees. Payment is due at or before pickup.

Late Returns: Returns made 30 minutes past the agreed time will incur a late fee plus an additional hourly usage charge.

Repo Rights: CarHop reserves the right to repossess the vehicle at the Guest's expense without notice if the vehicle is not returned on time or is used in violation of this Agreement.

3. DRIVER ELIGIBILITY & LIABILITY The Guest warrants they hold a valid driver's license. The Guest acknowledges that their personal auto insurance is the PRIMARY source of coverage for any liability, collision, or damage claims arising during the booking, to the full extent permitted by Connecticut law.

4. VEHICLE CONDITION & REPORTING The Guest accepts the vehicle in its current condition. The Guest must inspect the vehicle before driving and upload photos of any pre-existing damage to the CarHop App/System. Failure to report pre-existing damage constitutes acceptance of the vehicle as damage-free. Any new damage found upon return will be charged to the Guest.

5. MILEAGE, FUEL & CLEANING

Mileage: 50 miles per day are included. Excess mileage is charged at $1.20 per mile.

Fuel: The vehicle must be returned with the same fuel level as pickup. Refueling will incur the cost of gas plus a $10.00 convenience fee.

Cleaning: The vehicle must be returned in a clean condition. Smoking of any kind is strictly prohibited and will result in a $250.00 cleaning fee.

6. INSURANCE & DEDUCTIBLE (IMPORTANT) The Guest has selected a protection plan. This plan limits the Guest's financial responsibility for physical damage to the vehicle to the "Deductible" (formerly Franchise) amount specified in the booking. Exclusion: If the Guest violates any terms of this Agreement (e.g., drunk driving, unauthorized drivers), the protection plan is VOID, and the Guest is liable for 100% of damages.

7. PROHIBITED USES Using the vehicle for the following is strictly prohibited and voids all insurance protection: (a) Commercial use (Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, etc.); (b) Off-road driving; (c) Towing or pushing anything; (d) Driving by anyone not listed on the booking; (e) Commission of a crime.

8. GPS TRACKING DISCLOSURE The Guest consents to the use of GPS tracking devices on the vehicle. CarHop may use GPS data to locate the vehicle if it is not returned on time, stolen, or to assist in emergency roadside assistance.

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION & ARBITRATION Governing Law: This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Connecticut. Arbitration: ANY DISPUTE ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT SHALL BE RESOLVED BY BINDING ARBITRATION IN CONNECTICUT. THE GUEST WAIVES THE RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL OR TO PARTICIPATE IN A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT.

10. ACKNOWLEDGMENT By accepting the keys or clicking "I Agree," the Guest assumes full responsibility for the vehicle and agrees to indemnify and hold CarHop harmless from any claims, losses, or legal fees arising from the Guest's use of the vehicle.
